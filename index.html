Introduction

Norova ("we," "us," "our") operates a patient communication system that helps clinics in Canada capture missed calls, send appointment reminders, and manage patient bookings. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle personal information in compliance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canadian privacy laws.

What Information We Collect

We collect and process the following personal information on behalf of clinics:

  • Patient names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Appointment dates and times
  • Clinic booking and communication records

This information is collected when:

  • A patient calls the clinic
  • A patient fills out an online form or booking request
  • A patient communicates via text, email, or social media
  • The clinic enters patient information into the system

How We Use Your Information

We use patient information only for the purposes the clinic has authorized:

  • Sending appointment reminders (via text or email)
  • Capturing missed calls and notifying patients
  • Booking appointments and managing schedules
  • Sending appointment confirmations
  • Communicating with patients on behalf of the clinic

We do NOT:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use your information for marketing (unless the clinic requests it)
  • Store health records or medical information
  • Access or use your data for any purpose beyond what the clinic has authorized

Data Protection & Security

All patient information is protected using industry-standard security measures:

  • Data is encrypted during transmission and at rest
  • Access to patient data is restricted to authorized clinic staff only
  • Our systems use enterprise-grade infrastructure
  • We maintain secure backups of all data
  • We monitor our systems for unauthorized access attempts

Data Storage & Location

Patient information may be stored on servers located outside of Canada, including in the United States. These servers use the same encryption and security standards as Canadian data centers. By using Norova, you consent to your data being processed and stored in this manner.

Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request to see what personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate or incomplete information be corrected
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop receiving communications from the clinic at any time by replying "STOP" to text messages or contacting the clinic directly

To exercise any of these rights, contact the clinic directly or email: fizza@norova.ca

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain patient information as long as the clinic maintains an active account with Norova. When an account is closed, all patient data is securely deleted within 30 days, unless the clinic requests archival.

Third-Party Services

Norova uses the following trusted platforms to deliver our service:

  • GoHighLevel (CRM and communication platform)
  • Make.com (automation and integration)
  • Google My Business (for booking integration)

These platforms are subject to their own privacy policies and security standards. We have data processing agreements in place to ensure they comply with Canadian privacy requirements.

Clinic Responsibility

Clinics using Norova are responsible for:

  • Obtaining patient consent before their information is processed
  • Informing patients about how their data will be used
  • Complying with their own privacy obligations under PIPEDA
  • Responding to patient requests for data access or correction

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website.
